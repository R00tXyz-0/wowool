# Press Releases - Latest Company News - BNN Bloomberg.

On Sunday, September 20, Shelby’s will open the doors of its 50th location at 221 Henry Street in Brantford, and the brand is inviting the entire community to join the celebration. The grand opening will feature “Za Fest in Za West,” a free-entry community festival with inflatables, a reptile show, a live caricature artist, games, prizes and more, alongside opening-day offers that include 100 free Shawarma Boutines and 50% off wraps, bowls and signature items.
