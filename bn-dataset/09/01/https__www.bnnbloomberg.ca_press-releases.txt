# Press Releases - Latest Company News - BNN Bloomberg.

G Mining Ventures Corp. (“GMIN” or the “Corporation”) (TSX: GMIN, OTCQX: GMINF) is pleased to announce that the Toronto Stock Exchange (the “TSX”) has accepted the Corporation’s Notice of Intention to make a normal course issuer bid (“NCIB”) to purchase, for cancellation, up to an aggregate of 7,537,688 common shares of GMIN (“Common Shares”), representing approximately 2.54% of the Corporation’s issued and outstanding Common Shares, being 296,306,236 Common Shares as of August 25, 2026.
