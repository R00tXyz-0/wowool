# FAQs, Help and Support - BNN Bloomberg.

Welcome to the new BNN Bloomberg! Here are the answers to some questions you may have.

## Content .

How do I find categories and video content? .

Navigating our new platform is easy! We’ve simplified things by categorizing content under “Investing,” “Business” and “Markets” instead of having everything in one section. This way, you can quickly find the news you’re looking for.

Also, we’ve moved all the latest video content and your favourite shows under the “Video” tab, so you can easily access the most up-to-date videos and catch up on your favourite programs.

How can I access video content? .

Video content is now blended with articles throughout the content lists. Look for the “play” button to identify video content.

Live streaming of BNN Bloomberg and Bloomberg Television is still available.

Where can I find the news? .

Easily find the news you’re looking for by ordering your searches by newest or most relevant results. Popular Searches show trending topics among BNN Bloomberg users. Use Advanced Search to filter by date, category or author.

## Data .

Are there any changes with data? .

We still deliver the same great data offerings you’re used to, with a new, updated look:.

 * Stocks : Interday and intraday information, Stock Scorecard, and annual and quarterly reports .

 * Markets : Interday and intraday information, biggest gainers and losers of the day, and the Market digest .

## Schedule .

Where can I find the Guest schedule? .

Find the Guest schedule on the homepage or by clicking here: BNN Guest Schedule . 

## Support .

What browsers support BNNBloomberg.ca ? .

For the best experience on desktop, we recommend using one of these supported browsers:.

 * Firefox 81 and above .

 * Google Chrome 84 and above .

 * Microsoft Edge 18 and above .

 * Safari 13 and above .

If you’re using an older version, most issues can be fixed by upgrading your browser.

Internet Explorer 11 is not supported.

If you need help, you can visit whatismybrowser.com to check your browser and version. 

Can I use the BNN Bloomberg app while travelling within Canada and the U.S.? .

BNNBloomberg.ca and the BNN Bloomberg app can be used anywhere in Canada and around the world on your mobile device or computer. 

If you have any feedback, please reach out using our contact form .
