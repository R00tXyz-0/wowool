# Trade war: Dominic Barton is named chair of Invest in Canada.

Updated: August 31, 2026 at 4:44p.m. EDT .

Published: August 31, 2026 at 11:35a.m. EDT .

OTTAWA — Prime Minister Mark Carney is overhauling the leadership of the federal agency responsible for attracting foreign investment just weeks before a high-profile summit aimed at doing just that.

Carney announced today that Dominic Barton, the former Canadian ambassador to China and a well-known name in corporate Canada, will chair the board of directors for Invest in Canada.

Gurinder Grewal, an investment fund manager with experience in the energy and infrastructure sectors, will become chief executive of the agency.

Grewal replaces outgoing CEO Laurel Broten, a former Ontario cabinet minister.

Dominic LeBlanc, the minister responsible for the federal investment agency, announced on social media today that he would accept Broten’s resignation.

The shakeup at Invest in Canada comes two weeks before Carney’s inaugural investment summit in Toronto, where he’ll assemble foreign investors and the heads of major domestic funds to pitch them on Canadian projects.
