Title: Mark Carney talks Canada-U.S. trade with business leaders

# Mark Carney talks Canada-U.S. trade with business leaders.

Prime Minister Mark Carney has met with a number of business leaders across different sectors to discuss Canada’s relationship with United States as the trade war between the two countries rages on.

U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Monday that the United States was “not at war” with Canada, while U.S. President Donald Trump continued to push for renaming Lake Ontario “Lake America.”.

On Monday U.S. officials confirmed Trump had contacted Apple about the change. The development comes after a “Lake America” label appeared on several provincial government agency websites over the weekend, following a Google Maps update that changed how the lake’s name is displayed in the United States and internationally. 

By Monday morning, the label was back to “Lake Ontario” after officials at agencies including Metrolinx, Hydro One and the LCBO scrambled to reverse the change.

## MapQuest refuses to rename Lake Ontario .

MapQuest, the self-proclaimed “OG of online mapping,” will not be following the footsteps of its competitor Google. It is keeping Lake Ontario’s name, despite President Trump’s executive order to rename the Great Lake to “Lake America.”.

“We’re not changing it,” the company said in a post on social media on Friday.

Instead, the mapping company created a map generator for users to name Lake Ontario whatever they please.

“Name it whatever you want at your leisure,” MapQuest wrote, encouraging those who do to tag them so they can see the creativity. The mapping company shared an example, with “Lake Are We Doing This Again?” written over the lake on its map, in an apparent jab to when Trump renamed the Gulf of Mexico to the “Gulf of America.”.

Alex Arsenych , CTVNewsToronto.ca journalist .

## Que. government websites revert to Lake Ontario .

It’s Lake Ontario, as far as the Quebec government is concerned.

For a brief period on Monday, users visiting the provincial transportation ministry’s interactive traffic map Quebec511.info saw “Lake America” appear on their screen. 

A spokesperson said the name of the lake was changed because the map uses data from Google Maps.

“The issue has been corrected. The official name, ‘Lake Ontario,’ is now displayed,” wrote Émilie Lord, a transportation ministry spokesperson, in an email.

The Ministry of Transport isn’t the only government website to show the wrong name.

Quebec’s liquor control board told CTV News that it has asked its third-party provider “to make the correction on our website … to revert the name to Lake Ontario,” while the maps change also affected the website of the SQDC, the government-owned corporation that handles the sale of recreational cannabis.

## Google Maps announces name change .

Google Maps says it’s changing the way the lake’s name is displayed . The name “Lake Ontario” will be shown to Canadian users while Americans will see the new “Lake America” name. For users in the rest of the world, both names will now be displayed. 

“These updates follow our long-standing policy for bodies of water with names that vary from country to country, and are starting to roll out now,” Google said in an online statement .

## ‘It’ll get changed back’: former U.S. ambassador to Canada .

A former U.S. ambassador to Canada calls the Lake Ontario name change a “huge distraction” that will likely be reversed by a future administration.

“I actually think this is a huge distraction and the current president of the United States is a master of distraction,” Gordon Giffin told CTV News Channel on Monday.

“When the facts aren’t going well we have foolishness, and this is foolishness, and I encourage Canadian leaders and Canadian citizens to just move on with their lives. Ignore this.”.

Giffin, who served as ambassador to Canada between 1997 and 2001 under former U.S. president Bill Clinton, hopes negotiators “can ignore the noise” and hammer out a deal that benefits both countries.

“No serious thinker in the United States – Google aside – plans to change the way they refer to Lake Ontario,” Giffin added. “It’ll get changed back.”.

Daniel Otis , CTVNews.ca journalist .

## Toyota, Honda may foot the tariff bill: Reuters analysis .

U.S. President Donald Trump is targeting Ottawa with a proposed 50 per cent tariff on Canadian car imports — but Japan’s Toyota and Honda may end up footing the bill, according to an analysis from Reuters.

The two Japanese automakers account for more than three-quarters of all cars made in Canada. They could be forced to shutter some production lines if the tariffs go into effect on Jan. 1 as proposed, analysts said.

While a deal could still be reached, the timing of the U.S. tariffs couldn’t be worse, as Japanese automakers are being stung by competition from low-cost Chinese EVs in markets such as Southeast Asia, Europe and Latin America.

## Trump’s new tariffs on Canada raise legal questions .

In firing up a trade war with Canada, U.S. President Donald Trump turned to a 96-year-old statute so obscure that many trade lawyers didn’t even know it was still on the books. 

A week ago, Trump invoked Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930 to slap a 50 per cent tax on US$20 billion worth of Canadian imports. The move prompted dollar-for-dollar retaliation from Ottawa and strained already-tense relations between the neighbours and longtime allies.

The president’s Section 338 tariff authority has never been used, let alone tested in court. “This law is literally a blank canvas because it’s never been litigated,’’ said Ryan Majerus, a partner at King & Spalding and a former U.S. trade official.

The Associated Press .
