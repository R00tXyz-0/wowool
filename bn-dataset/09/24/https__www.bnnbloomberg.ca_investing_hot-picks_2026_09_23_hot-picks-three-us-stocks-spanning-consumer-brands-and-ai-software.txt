# SharkNinja, Dynatrace and Amer Sports lead three U.S. stock picks.

Consumer brands and AI software are drawing attention as the holiday shopping season approaches. The three U.S. picks span household appliances, software, and sports and outdoor brands.

BNN Bloomberg spoke with Ryan Modesto, CEO and portfolio manager at i2i Capital Management, about his picks: SharkNinja, Dynatrace and Amer Sports.

## Key Takeaways .

 * SharkNinja’s share price has held up while the broader consumer discretionary sector has struggled, a divergence Modesto views favourably. 

 * Modesto says SharkNinja’s growth depends on its ability to keep developing products that attract consumers. 

 * He sees potential for Dynatrace to grow as businesses seek tools to monitor how their AI applications behave. 

 * Modesto says Amer Sports’ improved outlook makes its recent share price decline look overdone. 

 * A consumer slowdown remains a risk for both consumer-focused picks, though Modesto expects holiday spending to hold up.
