# Press Releases - Latest Company News - BNN Bloomberg.

NioBay Metals Inc. (“NioBay” or the “Company”) (TSX-V: NBY) (OTCQB: NBYCF) was pleased to welcome, on Monday, August 31, representatives from the municipalities that make up the Maria-Chapdelaine RCM. Indeed, the mayors of the municipalities of Girardville, Saint-Thomas-Didyme, Normandin, Péribonka, Sainte-Jeanne-d’Arc, Saint-Eugène-d’Argentenay, and Saint-Stanislas were present for the visit.
