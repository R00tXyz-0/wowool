Title: Canada looks to strengthen its satellite industry

# Canada looks to strengthen its satellite industry.

Canada’s space sector is seeking global investment as it builds advanced communications infrastructure and expands its international reach. Industry leaders see the Canada Investment Summit as an opportunity to attract both financial and strategic partners.

BNN Bloomberg spoke with Daniel Goldberg, president and CEO of Telesat, about the investor response to the company’s pitch and the benefits that foreign capital could bring to Canada.

## Key Takeaways .

 * Telesat says its $7-billion Lightspeed constellation is fully funded, but additional capital could support further investment. 

 * The company plans to begin launching Lightspeed satellites next year and expects global coverage in early 2028. 

 * The network is being developed as an alternative to Starlink for defence, aviation, maritime and telecommunications customers. 

 * Strategic investors could provide customer relationships and domestic demand in addition to capital. 

 * Satellite communications could support Canadian sovereignty, rural broadband access, employment, innovation and exports.
