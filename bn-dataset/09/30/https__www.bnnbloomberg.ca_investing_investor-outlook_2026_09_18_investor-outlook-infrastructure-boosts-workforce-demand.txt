# Nation-building drives services growth.

Canada’s expanding pipeline of infrastructure, energy, mining, defence and data centre developments is increasing the need for services that support large and remote worksites.

BNN Bloomberg spoke with Trevor Haynes, chair and CEO of Black Diamond Group, about the company’s three business lines and its exposure to major projects in Canada, the U.S. and Australia.

## Key Takeaways .

 * Black Diamond has more than $2 billion in formal workforce solutions bids outstanding across over 20 Canadian projects, although the bids are not contracted revenue. 

 * The company has about 5,000 rooms of available capacity within its fleet of roughly 13,000 relocatable accommodation rooms. 

 * Potential demand spans LNG, oilsands, mining, critical minerals, military infrastructure, carbon capture and data centre construction. 

 * LodgeLink’s total trade value increased 69 per cent year over year in the second quarter as demand for workforce travel management grew. 

 * Black Diamond’s 45 Indigenous partnerships support employment and participation by Indigenous communities near remote projects.
