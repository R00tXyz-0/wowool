# Opinion - BNN Bloomberg.

Corporate earnings remain a powerful support for equities, but the bar for companies is getting higher. Resurging oil prices and elevated bond yields have brought inflation, valuations, and central-bank policy back into sharper focus, while strong earnings expectations leave less room for disappointment.
