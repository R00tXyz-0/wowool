# Mark Carney: Reaction to Canada-European Union alliance.

OTTAWA — In a historic address to the European Parliament on Thursday, Prime Minister Mark Carney said he welcomes European Council President Ursula von der Leyen’s ambition to see Canada become the first associate member of the EU.

He said while the name and nature of the new arrangement have yet to be determined, the partnership would go up for debate and a vote in Parliament.

Here are some of the reactions from Canada and abroad to the push for closer ties between the EU and Canada:.

## Prime Minister Mark Carney .

“The alliance between Canada and Europe is not defined by what we oppose. It is about what we are for: freedom, solidarity, prosperity, sustainability. An alliance strong enough so that no one can dictate our choices. Europe and Canada are each strong. Europe and Canada are even stronger together.”.

## Conservative Leader Pierre Poilievre .

“We need to put Canada first, in this time more than ever. The decisions for our country, though, must not be made in Washington, Beijing or Brussels, but by Canadians here at home … While we support free trade and continued NATO co-operation with our European allies, Conservatives say no EU taxes, no EU laws and no EU open border rules on immigration in Canada.”.

## European Parliament President Roberta Metsola .

“Europe and Canada share a deep friendship, rooted in a history that shaped who we are. Now, we’re breaking new ground. Expanding our alliance to boost trade, protect communities and improve lives. Together, we’re stronger.”.

## Nova Scotia Premier Tim Houston .

“Canada is a sovereign country. We will decide who we trade with and how we build our future. It’s of no surprise that President (Donald) Trump is now lashing out at the prospect of Canada increasing our trade ties with Europe. His response is next-level fear of missing out. He has an option: stop being a bully and maybe you’ll get invited back to the party. I am proud that Canada is spreading our wings … We will not be bullied by those who look to oppress us. My focus will continue to be on protecting our future and building Nova Scotia.”.

## Quebec Premier Christine Fréchette .

“Quebec cannot be a mere spectator in this new alliance. We are a distinct nation. We are a reliable and strategic economic partner, particularly in the sectors of clean energy, critical minerals, aerospace, defence, and artificial intelligence ... Today, I formally request of Mark Carney that Quebec obtain a special status in this alliance: a formal seat at the table and direct participation in discussions that affect our own jurisdictions as well as our interests. Quebec already holds a distinct place at the OIF and UNESCO. We must obtain comparable recognition in this new partnership.”.

## Sandro Gozi, European Democratic Party, European Parliament member .

“The speech delivered today by Prime Minister Mark Carney was historic, courageous and profoundly European in spirit. It set out a vision of the world that Europe largely shares and confirmed that Canada is more than a strategic partner: it is part of our wider democratic and political family. At a time when the international order is being reshaped by power politics, economic coercion and growing authoritarianism, Carney has shown the ambition and strategic clarity that this moment demands. Europe should respond with equal ambition.”.

## New Hampshire Sen. Jeanne Shaheen .

“Canada is now ‘aggressively’ shifting its trading elsewhere due to U.S. President Donald Trump’s trade war and insults by U.S. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick and others. Make no mistake: As New Hampshire’s most important trading partner, pushing Canada away will hurt Granite Staters.”.

This report by The Canadian Press was first published Sept. 17, 2026 .
